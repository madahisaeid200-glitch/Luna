# LUNA + LEO — Canonical Character Reference Pack Specification

Date: 2026-11-28  
Status: proposed specification; no reference image is approved or generated by this document.

## 1. Purpose and approval boundary

This specification defines how to build, compare, approve, version, and use the canonical visual identity packs for LUNA and LEO. Its purpose is to reduce face drift, body inconsistency, neck/face mismatch, hand defects, and the "different actor every shot" effect.

**No image becomes canonical through recency, assistant preference, filename, visual similarity, or inclusion in a contact sheet.** Approval must be an explicit user decision tied to an exact candidate ID and revision. Until then, all existing candidates remain historical or experimental and must not be supplied as authoritative image-to-video references.

This document authorizes preparation and controlled review only. It does not authorize paid generation, production video, provider selection, or automatic retries.

## 2. Identity constraints already established

### LUNA
- Adult woman, approximately 27 in presentation.
- Elegant, attractive, luxury-European styling.
- Long, glossy chestnut/dark-brown hair is the established direction.
- Beauty must remain natural rather than exaggerated or heavily retouched.
- Dark hair with light eyes was requested as an experiment, not approved as final eye color.

### LEO
- Adult man, handsome and attractive, with natural proportions and a refined luxury-European presentation.
- Masculine wardrobe and styling; never place him in women's clothing.
- Face, hair, eye color, exact age presentation, and body details remain unapproved until tied to a candidate and explicit approval.

### Couple continuity
- Both characters must remain independently recognizable in the same frame.
- No blending, swapping, or borrowing facial features, hair, body shape, or clothing between them.
- Romantic chemistry should come from expression, gaze, timing, and natural gestures—not distorted anatomy or exaggerated poses.

## 3. Candidate generation and comparison rules

1. Assign every candidate a unique immutable ID before review (for example, `LUNA-CAND-001` or `LEO-CAND-001`).
2. Generate candidates as separate single-character images. Do not begin with a couple collage or multi-panel contact sheet as the identity source.
3. Keep prompts, model/version, settings, date, and source image lineage with each candidate.
4. Change one major visual variable at a time. Do not simultaneously change face shape, hair, eye color, age presentation, wardrobe, and lighting.
5. Present a small, clearly labeled comparison set; do not silently replace a prior candidate.
6. Mark rejected candidates `REJECTED` and retain their IDs so they cannot re-enter production accidentally.
7. Do not describe an assistant-selected candidate as "the user's choice".

## 4. Required reference pack per character

The minimum pack is built from one approved identity, not independently generated unrelated views.

| ID suffix | Required image | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `-P01` | Neutral front head-and-shoulders portrait | Primary face identity anchor |
| `-P02` | Three-quarter portrait, turned about 30–45 degrees | Nose, cheek, jaw, and eye geometry |
| `-P03` | Side profile | Profile and face-to-neck continuity |
| `-B01` | Full-body front, relaxed neutral stance | Body proportions and posture |
| `-B02` | Full-body three-quarter stance | Depth, shoulder/hip proportions, silhouette |
| `-E01` | Subtle natural smile | Expression range without identity change |
| `-E02` | Neutral attentive expression | Baseline performance reference |

Optional after the minimum pack passes:
- A second natural expression (mild surprise or amusement).
- A controlled wardrobe variant.
- A couple reference frame made only from the two approved individual packs.

Do not use hand-on-cheek, hand-near-face, extreme perspective, dramatic profile shadows, sunglasses, hats, heavy filters, beauty retouching, or busy backgrounds in the core identity pack. Hands should be visible only where their anatomy can be inspected clearly.

## 5. Capture and rendering standards

Use consistent conditions across views:
- Photorealistic live-action appearance; visible natural skin texture and fine hair detail.
- Soft, neutral, even lighting with realistic shadows; no colored cinematic lighting in the core pack.
- Plain, uncluttered neutral background.
- Eye-level camera; natural lens perspective; avoid wide-angle facial distortion.
- Consistent distance, framing, exposure, and color balance.
- No text, watermark, collage layout, beauty filter, artificial skin smoothing, or excessive sharpening.
- Neutral, fitted but non-revealing clothing for body references; realistic anatomy and non-exaggerated proportions.
- Natural head/neck proportions and a clearly continuous jaw-to-neck transition.
- No props or accessories that obscure identity features.

Core face views should use the same hair styling and wardrobe. Any intentional hair or wardrobe variation is recorded as a separate optional variant, never as a replacement identity.

## 6. Identity anchors and allowed variation

For each approved character, record a concise identity sheet covering:
- Face silhouette and jaw/chin shape.
- Eye shape and approved eye color.
- Brow shape and spacing.
- Nose bridge/tip and width.
- Mouth/lip shape and smile characteristics.
- Hair color, hairline, length, texture, and parting.
- Skin tone and natural texture.
- Ear shape/visibility and distinguishing marks, if any.
- Adult age presentation.
- Height impression, shoulder/hip proportions, and build from full-body references.
- Voice identity only after a separate audio approval.

Classify attributes as:
- **LOCKED:** must remain stable across all scenes.
- **FLEXIBLE:** may vary within the approved range (expression, gaze, natural pose).
- **SCENE-DEPENDENT:** wardrobe, accessories, hairstyle arrangement, lighting, and environment may change only when the script calls for it.

Never infer an unapproved trait from a candidate image. If two approved views conflict, stop and resolve the reference pack before video testing.

## 7. Quality gates

### Image-pack gate
Every required view must pass:
- Same apparent person across all angles.
- No face geometry or eye-color drift.
- Plausible ears, teeth, hairline, neck, shoulders, hands, and body proportions where visible.
- Natural skin and hair detail without wax/plastic rendering.
- No hidden anatomy defects caused by cropping or occlusion.
- Consistent lighting and camera perspective.

### Video gate
After user approval of both packs, use the five controlled shots in `CHARACTER_REALISM_QA_PROTOCOL.md`. Begin with one portrait-stability shot and one two-person interaction shot. Continue to dialogue, full-body motion, and camera/lighting tests only if the first two pass.

A single hard failure—identity drift, face/neck mismatch, malformed hands/limbs, face blending, strong flicker/warping, or unnatural body movement—rejects that test regardless of average score.

## 8. Versioning, evidence, and registry

Use immutable pack versions such as `LUNA-REF-v1.0` and `LEO-REF-v1.0`. A new face, eye color, hairline, body proportions, or other locked attribute requires a new major version and renewed user approval. Minor revisions may correct metadata or replace a technically defective view only if the identity itself does not change; document the reason and preserve the previous version.

For every image, store:
- Candidate/asset ID and exact filename or Library file ID.
- Character and pack version.
- File SHA-256 digest when the bytes are available.
- Created date, model/version, prompt version, settings, and source lineage.
- Status: `EXPERIMENTAL`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, or `SUPERSEDED`.
- Exact approval evidence (user statement, date, and exact candidate ID).
- Known defects and permitted use.

The asset registry remains the source of truth. A candidate cannot be marked `APPROVED` without exact approval evidence. Never overwrite a previous approved file; create a new version and retain history.

## 9. Privacy, cost, and operational safeguards

- Do not expose access tokens, private account data, or credentials in prompts, filenames, metadata, or repository files.
- Do not upload user-private reference files to public repositories.
- Prefer local/low-cost candidate preparation where feasible; verify current route pricing before any paid request.
- Before every paid generation, record model, route, duration, resolution, estimated cost, and a hard budget ceiling.
- Default to one attempt. A retry requires a documented change; stop after two retries or at the budget ceiling, whichever comes first.
- No automatic paid provider failover.
- Preserve the original approved reference files and their hashes; do not recompress or edit the only canonical copy.
